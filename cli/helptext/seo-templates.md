# SEO & Sponsor Templates

GitMap provides built-in SEO and sponsor annotations for multi-repo consolidation, `commit-in`, and `commit-pull` workflows.

## Usage

```bash
gitmap commit-in <target> <inputs...> --sponsor
gitmap commit-pull <target> <inputs...> --seo-template riseup
```
